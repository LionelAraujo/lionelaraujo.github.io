What this app does

Mundo da Ciencia Posts is a private publishing tool. It uploads short vertical videos that we produce ourselves (science explainers, news summaries and original fiction) to our own TikTok account, @mundodaciencia18, using the TikTok Content Posting API. The account owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the app once. Videos are sent either as drafts to the creator's TikTok inbox, for the creator to review and post, or, when the creator chooses, published directly with the caption and privacy settings the creator selected.

The app is not offered to the public and does not collect content or data from other TikTok users.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), scope user.info.basic, to show which account is connected.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, so the app can upload videos (scopes video.upload and video.publish).
  • The publish ID and status of each upload, to confirm the video arrived.

How we use it

Only to upload our own videos to the connected account and to check the upload status. We do not read followers, messages, likes, viewing history or other users' data.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored in a private, access-restricted repository used only by the account owner's publishing workflow. They are never published, sold or shared with third parties.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data. Videos are sent only to TikTok.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the app is authorized. When access is revoked in TikTok, or on request, we delete the stored tokens.

For privacy requests, contact us through the Instagram account @escolhazero72.
